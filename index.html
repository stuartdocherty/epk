BIO

Stuart Docherty is a Guitarist, Composer and Sound Designer living in Stockholm, Sweden.

Originally from Scotland, Stuart has been playing guitar for more than 26 years across many different genres playing various models of left handed guitars.

With a classical music background and coming from roots in Scottish Folk Music he is known for fast, technical yet melodic guitar playing in European Power Metal.

Stuart holds a Master of Music in Sound Arts from the University of Aberdeen and also works as the Principle Sound Designer and Composer for Coffee Stain North in Stockholm, Sweden.

Stuart has also worked and collaborated with many other bands including Sharky Sharky, Fraser Edwards, Autumn's Mourning and Cult of Whispers.
